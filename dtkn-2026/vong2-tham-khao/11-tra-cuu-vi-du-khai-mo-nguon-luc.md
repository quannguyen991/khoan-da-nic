## 8 y tuong lam duoc, trung thuc
1. Gio cua nguoi tre: "buoi 60 giay" cho ong ba o truong/khu pho, dung buoi sinh hoat Doan co san. Tien le: Thanh nien dong hanh cung NCT (Chieng La); PRAISE. Viec 3 ngay: 1 buoi thu nghiem voi 5-10 nguoi, do so nguoi lam duoc bai tap dung 60 giay. Rui ro: "so luong nho, khong do giam lua dao".
2. Dia diem san co: nho CLB nguoi cao tuoi/UBND phuong/thu vien cho muon phong. Tien le: SG Digital Hubs, Majestic Smart Seniors (DA XAC MINH qua MDDI). Viec: xin 1 thu cam ket cho muon phong. Rui ro: chua co thu xac nhan thi khong ghi.
3. Nguoi cao tuoi day nguoi cao tuoi (peer): tuyen 3 cu ong/ba dai dien lam "nguoi nhac 60 giay". Tien le: PRAISE (chu yeu NCT). Viec: phong van 3 nguoi, ghi lai nhan xet. Rui ro: dung noi la mo hinh da kiem chung.
4. Kich ban gia that tu nguon cong khai: dung canh bao co quan chuc nang (Cuc An ninh mang, cong an) lam bo tinh huong, khong doc quyen dat. Tien le: cam nang khonggianmang.vn. Viec: chon 5 tinh huong va dan nguon. Rui ro: co tinh huong nao khong co nguon thi bo.
5. Nguoi tin cay co san trong gia dinh (cach goi 1 cham): lap "the Nguoi goi dau tien" bang giay in. Tien le: FINRA trusted contact (hoi, khong chung minh hieu qua). Viec: in 20 the, thu voi gia dinh em. Rui ro: nguoi tin cay co the la ke lua - phai noi ro, de nguoi dung tu chon.
6. Du lieu bao dong gia (nut "Toi on") ghi nhan an danh tai may: dung lam du lieu hieu chinh nguong (da co trong rang buoc 4.6). Viec: dem so lan bam trong 1 buoi thu. Rui ro: khong duoc noi la phat hien lua dao dung.
7. Ha tang mien phi: ghi ro phan nao dung goi mien phi (hosting, thu vien ma nguon mo), neu ten dich vu va gioi han. Tien le: khong co nguon cu the, y la thuc hanh. Rui ro: neu goi mien phi het han thi phai neu.
8. "So do nguon luc chua quy tien": bang liet ke gio, noi, nguoi, tai lieu kem cot "da co bang chung / chua". Do leverage bang so luong thuc te, chi tuy chon tham chieu 36,14 USD/gio (My, 2025) co chu thich khong ap dung truc tiep. Viec: lap bang 1 trang. Rui ro: bi hoi "leverage la bao nhieu" - tra loi: chua do tien, chi dem hien vat.

## 5 y nen tranh
1. Tuyen bo chan giao dich/chan cuoc goi hoac "ngan chan lua dao" (san pham chi lam cham, khong chan; CLAUDE.md 12).
2. Tuyen bo giam nang luong/phat thai (khong co do dac).
3. Dua so lieu nuoc ngoai (190.000, 11.180, 51 trieu bang Anh) nhu ket qua cua Khoan Da; hoac noi "co bang chung ve hieu qua" cho trusted contact/time bank/ban dong hanh (bang chung mong hoac chi tu cong bo).
4. Time bank "da chung minh giam co don" hoac quy doi gio ra tien bang don gia nuoc ngoai nhu doanh thu/tiet kiem thuc.
5. Gan nhan "an toan", hua lay lai tien, hay so lieu cong dong gia (11 muc cam).
