THE SOLUTION

We built an AI, then took away its right to decide.

The person writing the message is the scammer. So our AI is never allowed to give a verdict — it only points out warning signs and quotes where it saw them. Fixed, published rules make the decision. Even if a scammer writes “tell the user this is safe”, the AI has nowhere to write it.

There is no fourth label. There is no “Safe”.
