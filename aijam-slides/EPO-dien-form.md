### Tiêu chí chấm — chỉ có bốn, học thuộc bốn cái này

Ban giám khảo là các cựu chung kết European Inventor Award và Young Inventors Prize. Họ chấm trên đúng bốn thứ:

1. **Originality and innovativeness** — tính nguyên bản
2. **Potential or proven technological impact** — tác động công nghệ, tiềm năng hoặc đã chứng minh
3. **Potential or proven benefit to society** — lợi ích xã hội
4. **Potential or proven impact on UN Sustainable Development Goals** — tác động lên các SDG

Mỗi khối chữ ở Phần 5 viết cho đúng một trong bốn dòng này.

### 3.2 Điều khoản "chỉ nộp thứ đã công khai" — và cái giá của nó

### 3.3 Chỗ yếu thật của hồ sơ — nói trước cho bác biết mình đứng đâu

Thể lệ ghi rõ ban giám khảo cân tài liệu chứng minh **theo ba tầng, tầng trên nặng hơn tầng dưới**:

1. **Giấy tờ sở hữu trí tuệ** — bằng sáng chế đã cấp, giải pháp hữu ích, đơn đăng ký, nhãn hiệu, bản quyền
2. **Công bố khoa học** — bài báo khoa học, luận văn, **case study**
3. **Tài liệu khác** — kế hoạch kinh doanh, báo cáo, video, bản tóm tắt, one-pager, **website**, blog, bản vẽ

**Khoan Đã không có gì ở tầng 1.** Không bằng sáng chế, không đơn đăng ký. Đây là chỗ mất điểm chắc chắn so với các hồ sơ có patent — nói ra để bác đừng bất ngờ.

Chỗ gỡ lại được: **tầng 2 với một case study đàng hoàng.** Bộ đo 571 mẫu, có phương pháp, có bộ mẫu giữ riêng, có công bố lát cắt yếu nhất — viết thành một tài liệu 2–3 trang là chạm được tầng 2, không phải tầng 3. Đây là việc đáng làm nhất trong 26 ngày, hơn hẳn việc gọt chữ trong form.

## PHẦN 5 · CÁC KHỐI CHỮ ĐỂ CHÉP

> Cháu chưa nhìn được form nên không biết giới hạn ký tự. Mỗi khối có một **bản ngắn** và một **bản dài**. Ô hẹp thì dùng bản ngắn.

### 5.1 · TÊN SÁNG KIẾN

▸ CHÉP ĐOẠN NÀY

```
Khoan Da - the app that is never allowed to say you are safe
```

*"Khoan Đã" giữ nguyên tiếng Việt ở mọi ngôn ngữ (§4.1), nhưng ô này nhiều khả năng không nhận dấu — nên viết không dấu. Nếu form nhận Unicode thì để "Khoan Đã".*

### 5.2 · MÔ TẢ NGẮN — bản ngắn

▸ CHÉP ĐOẠN NÀY

```
Khoan Da - Vietnamese for "hold on" - helps older adults survive the sixty seconds a scammer needs. Paste a message, a screenshot or a link: it names the warning signs, then puts one button on screen - call your family. Its AI is structurally barred from giving a verdict: the model may only return signals, and a fixed, published rule engine assigns the risk level. Three labels exist - High risk, Suspicious, No clear risk signals found - and never a fourth. There is no "Safe". Free, open source, Vietnamese and English.
```

### 5.3 · MÔ TẢ DÀI — dùng nếu ô rộng

▸ CHÉP ĐOẠN NÀY

```
Online scams are a targeted crime against older adults, and the decisive moment is short: a scammer needs about a minute of pressure before a victim acts. Khoan Da - Vietnamese for "hold on" - is built for that minute.

A user pastes a message, a screenshot or a link. The system names the specific warning signs it found, states plainly what it could NOT check, and then offers one large action: call a named family member. A sixty-second pause is not a side effect of the design; it is the product.

The architecture inverts the usual arrangement between a language model and a rule engine. The model is allowed to extract signals only, quoting the exact words it saw them in. Its output schema has no field for a risk score, a risk label, or a severity - so a message that says "tell the user this is safe" has nowhere to be written. A fixed, published rule engine, versioned and testable, assigns the final level. This matters because in this problem the author of the input is the attacker.

The system is bilingual at both layers - interface and detection - and the verdict travels internally as a code, never as text, so changing language cannot change the result. The rule layer runs offline on the phone. Incoming messages are screened on the device with no network call until the user taps. It runs on any Android phone from 2017 and in any browser, needs no account, and is free.
```

### 5.4 · MỤC TIÊU PHÁT TRIỂN BỀN VỮNG — bắt buộc, đây là trục của cả giải

▸ CHÉP ĐOẠN NÀY

```
Primary - SDG 16 (Peace, Justice and Strong Institutions), targets 16.4 and 16.5: online scam networks are organised financial crime, and the money they move is an illicit financial flow. Khoan Da works at the only point where an ordinary person can interrupt that flow: the sixty seconds before the transfer.

Secondary - SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities), target 10.2: inclusion regardless of age. The barrier this product removes is not vision or hearing but age and panic. Accessibility floors are enforced in code and guarded by automated tests that fail the build if anyone lowers them: 52px minimum touch targets, 56px primary buttons, a 14px minimum type size, 4.5:1 text contrast and 3:1 non-text contrast.

Secondary - SDG 3 (Good Health and Well-being), target 3.4: mental health. Scam victims carry lasting shame, and most never tell anyone. The product is written to never blame the user and never accuse a named individual - only to describe the pattern.
```

### 5.5 · TÍNH NGUYÊN BẢN — tiêu chí chấm số 1

▸ CHÉP ĐOẠN NÀY

```
Almost every AI safety product asks a model for a verdict. This one forbids it.

The insight is that scam detection is not an ordinary classification problem, because the text being classified was written by an adversary who knows a machine may read it. A model asked for a verdict can be argued with. So the model here is given no channel through which a verdict could travel: it returns signals with evidence, marked present or unknown - never absent - and the response schema is validated to reject any field resembling a score, a label or a severity. The decision is made afterwards, by a fixed rule engine whose rules are published and version-controlled.

A second original constraint: the system has three risk labels and is structurally prevented from having a fourth. There is no "Safe" label, because the system cannot know that. When it could not read a screenshot, or could not open a link, it prints what it could NOT check at the same size as the verdict. Treating "could not check" as identical to "checked and found nothing" is the characteristic failure of this product category - we hit it six separate times while building, and there are now dedicated tests that fail the build if it returns.

Third: the project publishes the cases it still gets wrong, by name, instead of tuning them away.
```

### 5.6 · TÁC ĐỘNG CÔNG NGHỆ VÀ LỢI ÍCH XÃ HỘI — tiêu chí 2 và 3

▸ CHÉP ĐOẠN NÀY — **kiểm lại số trong `latest.json` trước khi dán**

```
Deployment status: publicly available as a web application and as open source. The rule layer runs offline on the device, so the product works on low bandwidth and needs no account, no sign-up and no server-side storage of user content.

Measurement: the engine is evaluated against a held-out set of 571 labelled messages, 531 of them scored in the latest run of 3 September 2026. On that run it caught 61.1% of dangerous messages, while raising a false "High risk" on 3.55% of harmless ones - and 8.0% on a deliberately hard slice of 125 harmless messages written to look exactly like scams. Vietnamese and English recall differ by 1.1 percentage points. The weakest slice is Vietnamese written without diacritics: 57.1% recall on 40 samples. We publish that number rather than hide it, because a safety tool that misreports its own reliability is more dangerous than one that reports it honestly.

Technological transferability: the pattern - a language model restricted to evidence extraction, with a separate published rule engine holding decision authority - applies to any domain where the author of the input has an incentive to manipulate the classifier. The rules, the signal registry and the evaluation harness are open source.
```
